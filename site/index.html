How much should a corner shop order every Monday and Thursday?

Sold-out days hide real demand. A censored demand model recovers it, and an order rule built on that forecast is compared with the owner's current rule on the same six months.

How the decision was made

  1. Simulated stores with day-of-week, payday, December and price effects.
  2. Negative binomial demand with stock-outs treated as censored observations.
  3. Out-of-sample validation against simple forecasts.
  4. Service level per product chosen by simulation, with a bootstrap interval of the saving.

What it does not claim

  • Synthetic data; figures do not belong to any real business.
  • Assumes lost sales without substitution.
  • A real store needs its own parameters.

Analysis notebook and full evidence in the repository.
